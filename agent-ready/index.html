What just happened

The buyer just changed

On October 6, 2026, Sierra & Meta announced the Personal Agent Protocol, an open standard for how personal AI agents deal with businesses. Genesys, Instinct, Rocket, Shopify, Stripe & Walmart are building it with them.

In plain English… your customers are starting to send AI agents to do their shopping, booking & comparing for them. Now the big platforms are agreeing on how those agents knock on your door.

The v0.1 spec is due later this month. Payments (an agent completing a purchase without handling a card number) are on their roadmap, not live yet. You have a window right now. It won't stay open long.

I spent 20 years & over $100M of my own ad spend learning how to get a human to stop scrolling. An agent doesn't scroll. It reads, compares & picks whoever is clearest & easiest to do business with. This playbook is how we're getting our partners ready for that.

How it works

How an agent actually shops

Based on how the protocol is designed, here's the path an agent takes through your business:

personal_agent.log
  1. discover your site & what you offer # can it even tell what you sell?
  2. start session as a guest # no login yet
  3. read pricing, availability, return policy # the stuff you hide behind "call us"
  4. compare you vs. the other 2-3 options # your ad never enters the picture
  5. act sign in with the customer's OK & place the order # or move on to the next vendor

It can do that through your regular website, through an API, or by talking to your own company agent if you have one. Every step is a spot where you either get picked or get skipped. And nobody tells you when you got skipped.

Next 30 days

The 30-day plan

Don't try to fix all of it at once. Here's the order I'd run it in.

Week 1

Run the agent test

Ask 2 or 3 different AI assistants to find & buy what you sell. Screenshot everything. Where did they get stuck? Who did they pick instead?

Week 2

Fix the dead ends

Put real pricing on the site. Rewrite your return & cancellation terms in plain English. Kill any "call us" step that doesn't need to exist.

Week 3

Make it readable

Rewrite the top of every offer page for clarity. Add structured data. Build out the boring FAQ. Get your info consistent everywhere.

Week 4

Get on the protocol radar

Read the v0.1 spec when it drops. If you're on Shopify or Stripe, watch for what they ship. Ask your dev team what an API path for orders would take.

What doesn't change

Humans still send the agent

Your brand still matters. A person decides to send the agent, & they often tell it where to start looking. That's still won with great marketing, real proof & a reputation people talk about.

What changes is the last mile. The human gets you on the shortlist. The agent decides who gets the order. Most companies are only built for the first half of that.
